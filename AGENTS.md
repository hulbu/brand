# Hulbu brand — rules for every agent

This repo is **public** (`github.com/hulbu/brand`): the open home of Hulbu's logo, colours, favicons and social
media assets. Anyone (designers, press, partners, our own apps) should find what they need in under a minute.
Organise it like an open brand kit, not like a working folder.

## The logo (decided by the founder, 2026-10-04 — do not redesign it)
Three **equal, perfect circles** in a row, overlapping. Stacking: left = back, centre = middle, right = front.
Each front circle carries a transparent **gap ring** that cuts into the circles behind it (real negative space —
the background shows through; never white strokes).

Exact geometry (units of the 1080 design canvas; reproduce it exactly):
- radius R = 150; centres at x = 435 / 540 / 645, y = 540 (centre spacing 105)
- gap ring = 22 units around the middle and the front circle (radius 172), cutting the circles behind
- small sizes need wider gaps or the cuts vanish: **32 units at 32 px, 44 units at 16 px**
- the mark's bounding box is 510 × 300 units

Colours "Sunny sky": back **Sunny yellow `#FFE066`** · middle **Teal `#4ECDC4`** · front **Coral `#FF6B6B`**.
**Primary colour = Coral `#FF6B6B`** (the founder calls it "orange"; keep it as one token so it can be changed).
Ink `#2E2C33` · White `#FFFFFF` · Canvas "Cloud Dancer" `#F4F0EC`.

The current hand-made favicon set and the mask-based source SVGs are in `source/favicon-from-studio/` (`src.svg` =
the mark, `src-16.svg`/`src-32.svg` = the small-size cuts with wider gaps, `favicon.svg` = turns white in dark mode).
They were generated by `~/Development/ribblerio/ribbler-studio-2/public/projects/hulbu-logo/sheet.mjs` and
`favicon.mjs` (read them for the exact construction).

Reference for what a complete kit looks like (ours for Ribbler — we want to do better):
`~/Development/ribblerio/ribbler/logo/` (README, generators, favicons, social covers, website assets).

## Rules
- Every deliverable is **generated by a script in `source/`** (Node or Python), so a colour change regenerates the kit.
- Final SVGs: plain paths only (no masks, no filters, no `<text>`), integer viewBox, `<title>`.
- **Social media profile pictures: no outside border / ring / frame** around the logo. Just the mark on its background.
- Monochrome versions: **primary coral** (one colour on light), **ink** (one colour on light), **white** (on dark).
- Look at everything you render (open the PNGs). Test favicons at true 16/32 px.
- Never commit or push — the coordinator reviews and commits. Keep `STATUS.md` updated (what's done, what's next,
  `BLOCKED:` lines, `DONE` on the last line when finished).
