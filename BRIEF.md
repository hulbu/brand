# Brief for the brand agent (from the coordinator, 2026-10-04)

You are **brand**. Read `AGENTS.md` first (the logo, its exact geometry, colours and rules). Then build the complete,
open Hulbu brand kit in this repo. The founder is away; work autonomously, make sensible decisions, write them down.

## Deliverables
1. **`logo/`** — the mark as clean path-only SVGs (no masks), integer viewBox, `<title>`:
   - `hulbu-mark.svg` (Sunny sky colours), `hulbu-mark-coral.svg` (one colour, primary coral), `hulbu-mark-ink.svg`,
     `hulbu-mark-white.svg` (for dark backgrounds).
   - Small-size cuts of each: `*-32.svg` (gap 32) and `*-16.svg` (gap 44).
   - PNG exports of each at 512, 1024 and 2048 px wide (transparent).
   - No wordmark yet (the founder hasn't chosen a typeface) — leave a note in the README that it's pending.
2. **`favicon/`** — `favicon.svg` (colour), `favicon-auto.svg` (colour in light mode, white in dark mode via
   `prefers-color-scheme`), `favicon.ico` (16/32/48, each from its tuned cut), PNG 16/32/48, `apple-touch-icon.png`
   (180, on white), `android-chrome-192.png`, `android-chrome-512.png`, `maskable-512.png` (mark inside the 80 % safe
   zone on white), `site.webmanifest`, and `head.html` (the snippet to paste).
3. **`social/profile/`** — square profile pictures, **no outside border/ring/frame**, mark centred and safe for the
   circular crop every platform applies (mark inside the inscribed circle with margin). Variants: colour mark on
   white, colour mark on Cloud Dancer, white mark on coral, white mark on ink. Master 1080 × 1080 plus the platform
   sizes (Instagram 320, LinkedIn 400, YouTube 800, X 400, Facebook 720, TikTok 200) — check current
   recommendations with web search and note sources.
4. **`social/covers/`** — background/header images built from the three colours and geometric figures (circles,
   half circles, crescents echoing the mark), white or very light base, joyful and clean. For each: a **background
   only** version and a **with logo** version, respecting each platform's safe area and avatar overlap:
   - Open Graph 1200 × 630 (`og-default.png`) + 2–3 OG background variants
   - LinkedIn company cover 1128 × 191 and personal banner 1584 × 396
   - YouTube channel banner 2560 × 1440 (content inside the 1546 × 423 safe area)
   - X header 1500 × 500 · Facebook cover 1640 × 624 (check current sizes, note sources)
5. **`colors/`** — `tokens.json`, `tokens.css` (custom properties), `tokens.ts`, plus a swatch image with HEX/RGB/
   CMYK and WCAG contrast of each colour on white and on ink.
6. **`README.md`** — the public brand page: preview images, what's in the kit (table with links), colours, clear
   space and minimum sizes, do/don't (with small images), how to regenerate. Write it for an outsider.
   Add a `LICENSE` that keeps the logo a trademark while letting people use the files to refer to Hulbu; explain it
   in two lines in the README.
7. **`preview.html`** — one page showing every asset (for the coordinator's review).
8. **`source/`** — every file above is generated by scripts here; one command (`npm run build` or
   `python3 source/build.py`) regenerates the whole kit. Keep `source/favicon-from-studio/` as the reference.

## Quality bar
- Geometry and colours exact (compare your mark against `source/favicon-from-studio/src.svg` at the same size).
- Look at every PNG you render. Favicons crisp at true 16 and 32 px. No borders on profile pictures.
- Covers: nothing important under the avatar or outside safe areas; the logo never stretched or recoloured off-brand.

Keep `STATUS.md` updated; last line `DONE` when finished. Never commit or push.
